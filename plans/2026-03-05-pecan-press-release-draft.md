# ST. JUDE CLOUD PRESS RELEASE
## St. Jude Cloud Transforms Pediatric Cancer Research from Months to Minutes with New PeCan Capabilities

MEMPHIS, TN — [DATE TBD], 2026 — St. Jude Cloud today announced major enhancements to PeCan, the Pediatric Cancer Knowledgebase, enabling researchers to dynamically build custom cohorts from 11,500 pediatric cancer samples and stratify them across five integrated data facets—variants, gene expression, epigenetics, mutational signatures, and histology—entirely in the browser. These foundational improvements to data filtering, cohort building, and comparative analysis capabilities reimagine how pediatric cancer researchers explore and analyze complex genomic data.

Researchers can now define cohorts on the fly based on patterns they observe in one data modality, then immediately stratify those samples across other facets to investigate what's driving the differences. This kind of iterative, hypothesis-generating exploration—previously requiring months of data preparation and custom analysis pipelines—can now be accomplished in a single browser session. For example, a researcher can identify an unexpected cluster in a t-SNE expression visualization, select those samples to create a new cohort, then immediately stratify by mutational signatures to see if they share a common etiology—or pivot to histology to compare tumor morphology. Each question leads naturally to the next, with the data already at hand.

"This unlocks a completely different way of doing science," said [NAME], [TITLE]. "You see something interesting, build a cohort around it, stratify across other data modalities, and chase the thread immediately. That kind of rapid, iterative hypothesis generation just wasn't possible when every question meant weeks of data preparation and analysis."

PeCan provides access to the world's largest integrated pediatric cancer dataset—11,500 samples spanning [NUMBER] diagnoses, curated specifically for the unique biology of childhood cancers. The platform includes rare tumor types underrepresented in adult-focused resources like TCGA, alongside expertly curated cohorts defined by St. Jude scientists—who pioneered many of the disease classifications used worldwide—providing a well-characterized starting point for exploration.

## New Cohort Features Enable Real-Time Discovery

The intuitive cohort creation process guides researchers through naming their cohort, adding descriptions, and choosing whether to save as a new cohort or add samples to an existing one. Each creation is confirmed with detailed screens showing essential information—including Sample IDs, diagnosis category, and other metadata details—ensuring researchers have complete transparency about the composition of their cohorts before finalizing.

Once a cohort is created, researchers can seamlessly explore data across all five data facets. Scientists can identify patterns in one data type, then immediately investigate those patterns across others in a single workflow. In addition to user-created cohorts, PeCan offers pre-defined, expertly curated cohorts defined by St. Jude scientists, providing vetted entry points for new researchers or those exploring established classifications.

A new comparison mode allows scientists to view two cohorts simultaneously across all data visualization tools. The cohort selector makes it simple to choose which cohorts to compare, and researchers can easily switch the position of cohorts in the comparison view to understand what distinguishes one group from another. Each data facet has been optimized with appropriate layouts for comparison viewing and displays data availability information across all facets, allowing researchers to easily navigate to the most relevant datasets for their analysis.

## Enhancing the Research Experience

PeCan has been further enhanced with a new central dashboard serving as a dedicated workspace where researchers can manage their cohorts and track their explorations. Improved navigation throughout the application streamlines access to analysis tools and data facets, allowing researchers to focus on discovery rather than navigation.

"These updates reflect our commitment to removing barriers between researchers and the questions they want to answer," said [NAME], [TITLE]. "By accelerating the hypothesis generation cycle, we're enabling faster progress toward understanding pediatric cancers and ultimately, better treatments for children."

## Availability

The new cohort features are now available to all PeCan users at no cost. Researchers can access PeCan through St. Jude Cloud at https://pecan.stjude.cloud.

## About St. Jude Cloud

St. Jude Cloud is a collaborative data-sharing ecosystem that provides researchers worldwide with access to the largest pediatric cancer genomics datasets in the world. Through its suite of applications—including the Genomics Platform, PeCan, and Visualization Community—St. Jude Cloud supports open science and accelerates discoveries for children with cancer and other catastrophic diseases. Learn more at https://stjude.cloud.

## About PeCan

PeCan (Pediatric Cancer Data Portal) is St. Jude Cloud's interactive exploration platform, enabling researchers to dynamically explore processed genomic, transcriptomic, and epigenomic data from pediatric cancer samples without requiring data downloads or computational infrastructure. The platform serves as a critical resource for the global pediatric cancer research community.
